Research Interests

My research develops human-centric AX (AI Transformation) methods that connect computational modeling with strategic decision-making in business and public policy. One line of work focuses on Trustworthy AI: explainable and statistically verified models, including generative AI, whose outputs can be checked before they are used. Another applies causal inference to estimate which factors change business and policy outcomes. Across both, the goal is AI that decision makers can examine and rely on.
